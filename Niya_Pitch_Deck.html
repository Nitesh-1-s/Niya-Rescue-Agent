The Playbook

Three Targeted Interventions

Silent Retry

ERROR: BANK_DOWNTIME

When the AI detects a temporary bank crash, it avoids scaring the customer with an error. It safely schedules a background retry for 15 minutes later to save the sale seamlessly.

Send Discount SMS

ERROR: INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS

If a customer abandons a cart or lacks funds, the AI acts as a closer. It immediately fires a webhook to send a personalized WhatsApp message with a 5% discount, enticing them to use a different card.

Escalate to Human

ERROR: FRAUD_FLAG

Proving it operates within safe boundaries, the AI immediately halts execution when it detects high-risk edge cases (like suspected fraud). It compliantly escalates the ticket to human Ops.
